Sequential ramps (three measures each)

Every ramp is monotonic in lightness, so bins stay ordered under deuteranopia, protanopia and grayscale. Swatches show 100% and at the 74% map opacity over the light basemap. Share over $200K is well behaved and gets six equal-interval bins with no outlier class.

Outlier and no-data treatments
1lNear-black outlier with inner keyline · hatched no-data
Outlier reads as "beyond the scale", not as the top bin. Hatching can't be confused with the lightest bin. Hatch drawn as a MapLibre fill-pattern sprite.
1mTop-bin color + stipple · flat gray no-data with outline
Keeps the ramp to six colors; texture marks the extreme. Gray no-data is cheaper to render but sits closer to the light bins.
1nOutlier as heavy outline + label · no-data as empty outline
75205
Names outliers on the map itself. Strongest for "check the rich suburbs I know", but labels crowd when several outliers touch.

Center markers and trails
1rFilled vs hollow, year beads, dashed population trail
Beads = one per year, hover shows year + coords. Shape and fill carry identity, so it survives colorblindness and grayscale.
1sDiamond vs square, chevrons show direction
Direction reads without hovering. Chevrons every 4 years; best when trails are short and wiggly.
1tComet: trail thickens toward latest year · 1b palette
Time encoded in width and opacity; hover state shown on 2017. Hardest to read exact years, strongest at a glance.
